OurNet is a friend-to-friend app for chatting, planning and sharing with the people you trust, without a central platform in between.

You have one identity that your friends recognise, across your phone and PC. Everything you receive is signed, so you can see who wrote it and who passed it along.

What you can do:

• Chat one-to-one and in private groups, with voice and video calls
• Discuss things in forums for your groups
• Plan together with personal and shared group calendars
• See friends on a map when they choose to share where they are
• Share files and photos, and keep a private drive in sync across your devices
• Keep notes and checklists, with voice notes and home screen widgets
• Add apps and games as plugins, such as a wiki, a microblog and noughts and crosses

How it works:

OurNet connects your devices to your friends' devices directly. There is no account to sign up for and no OurNet server holding your messages. Devices encrypt what they send; when a direct connection is impossible, traffic may pass through a relay that cannot read it.

This is an early release. Expect rough edges, and please send feedback.
